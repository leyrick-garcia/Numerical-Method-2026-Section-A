📖 Glossary of Terms

TermDefinition
Area under the curveHow high multiplied by how long, summed. Here in m·h; divide by time span to get mean stage.
Central differenceEstimating a rate using the reading before and after. More balanced than forward/backward.
Degrees of freedomReadings minus parameters (n−p). Remaining independent evidence after fitting.
Derivative (dh/dt)Rate of change of level. First derivative: speed; second derivative: acceleration.
Finite differenceRate from two readings: subtract, divide by time. No model needed, but amplifies rounding.
IntegrationAdding area under a curve. Opposite of differentiation; smooths noise.
Least squaresMinimise sum of squared residuals. Makes large misses count more.
Levenberg‑Marquardt (LM)Iterative optimizer for curve fitting. Needs a good initial guess.
Logistic / sigmoidS‑shaped curve: flat, rise, flat again. Cannot go back down, hence two logistics used.
Moving averageSmooths by averaging neighbours. Cheap, but flattens genuine peaks.
p‑valueProbability that a parameter is actually zero. Low p (<0.05) means significant.
ParameterUnknown number in model equation solved by fit (c, a, k, t, etc.).
R²Fraction of variance explained. No units; can be high even if model is wrong.
ResidualObserved − predicted. Pattern in residuals reveals model shortcomings.
s (std error of estimate)Typical size of a residual, in metres. More informative than R².
Savitzky‑GolaySmooths by fitting a polynomial locally. Preserves peaks better than moving average.
SE (standard error)Uncertainty of a fitted parameter. Small SE = well determined.
Sign runsNumber of times residuals switch sign. Far fewer than expected indicates patterned misfit.
SSE / SSTSSE: sum of squared errors; SST: total sum of squares. R² = 1 − SSE/SST.
t‑statisticParameter value divided by its SE. Large t means parameter is not zero.
Trapezoid ruleIntegrate by joining points with straight lines. Used as cross‑check.
